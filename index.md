# CheckRates Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

CheckRates ("the app") is a currency converter published by Tianmu Creations. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: **we do not collect, store, or see your personal data.** The app has no accounts and no servers of its own.

## What stays on your device

- Your favorite currencies, converter settings, alerts, and preferences are stored only on your device.
- Your 7-day trial status is stored in encrypted storage on your device.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

## Exchange rates

To refresh exchange rates, the app contacts public rate services over the internet: open.er-api.com, api.frankfurter.dev (European Central Bank reference rates), cdn.jsdelivr.net, and api.coingecko.com. These services receive your IP address and standard technical request data, as with any internet request. They do not receive your name, email, or any identifier that identifies you. We recommend reading their own privacy policies if you want details.

## Advertising (free version only)

The free version of CheckRates shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers and interaction data to deliver and measure ads. You can learn more or opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings (Privacy → Tracking on iOS; Google → Ads on Android) or at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

## Purchases

CheckRates Pro ($5.00 per year, auto-renewing) is processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Purchase and subscription status is handled by the store on your device.

## Children

CheckRates is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. To erase everything the app stored on your device, uninstall the app. For questions, contact us at scalptheticker@gmail.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
